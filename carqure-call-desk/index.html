            How the score works

            Out of 100, using only the days each agent logged in. Productivity and connect rate are compared with the agent's own team, so Feedback and Customer Champion agents aren't judged against each other.

            • Productivity 30%: calls handled per logged-in hour. The team median scores 75; a third above the median scores 100.
            • Login adherence 20%: share of days at 7h 30m or more (amber days count half).
            • Outbound connect 15%: connect % against the team's. Team level scores 75.
            • Missed inbound 15%: 100 minus 10 points for each missed call per day.
            • AHT 10%: share of days at 2:00 or under (2:00–2:30 counts half).
            • Break + lunch 10%: share of days at 1h 15m or under (up to 1h 30m counts half).
